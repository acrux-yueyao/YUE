Designing for the long
work of becoming. 为漫长的
“成为”而设计。

I build strange but genuinely working artifacts — a deity you summon, a hall of AI clerks, a room of small screens — and take each one seriously as a way to ask a research question about how our values, self, and understanding keep shifting across a life. 我造一些奇怪、却真的能运行的作品——一位可被召唤的神明、一座 AI 办事大厅、一屋子小小的屏幕——再把每一个当作提出研究问题的方式：价值、自我与理解如何在一生中不断变化。

About关于

I’m an HCI researcher and designer at Boston University, working across interaction design, interactive art, and research through design.我是波士顿大学的 HCI 研究者与设计师，工作横跨交互设计、交互艺术与研究性设计。

I’m interested in the long work of becoming — how our values, sense of self, and understanding of the world keep shifting across a life — and in how design might make that negotiation gentler, more legible, and more expressible, without dictating where anyone should land.我关注漫长的“成为”——价值、自我认知与世界理解如何不断变化——以及设计的体验如何让这场协商更温和、更可被理解、更可被表达，同时不去规定任何人最终该落在哪里。

I’m currently preparing work toward CHI and applying to HCI PhD programs.目前正在为 CHI 投稿做准备，并申请 HCI 博士项目。

01

2026 · Playable Game · ~3-min demo可玩游戏 · 约 3 分钟 demo

GOD SHIFT

神谕之镜

A hand-painted narrative game. You’re the night-shift clerk at Shunyi (顺意), the complaint desk for an AI oracle. Three days, three cases — and each day the god answers a little more like you, until the redacted name on the last complaint turns out to be your own handwriting. A playable demo about why we want AI to seem like it understands us. From tool, to oracle, to mirror.一款手绘叙事游戏。你是「顺意」——一个 AI 神谕——投诉窗口的夜班文员。三日三关，神每天都更像「你」在说话，直到最后一封投诉里被涂黑的名字，正是你自己的笔迹。一个可玩的 demo，追问我们为何渴望 AI「看起来懂自己」。从工具，到神谕，到镜子。

You stare at the redacted name — but you recognize the handwriting. It’s yours.你盯着那行涂黑的名字——可你认得这笔迹。那是，你的字。
